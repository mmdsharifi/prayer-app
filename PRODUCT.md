# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Persian-speaking Muslims, developers, students, and professionals working on macOS and Web who seek a serene, distraction-free spiritual companion for prayer times, Jalali/Hijri calendar awareness, daily Quran juz recitation, and authentic Azkar & Hadith.

## Product Purpose

اذکار من provides real-time prayer schedule tracking, countdowns to upcoming prayers, astronomical solar-time ambient background phases, and daily Islamic reflections. It seamlessly integrates into the macOS top Menu Bar and offers an interactive, tactile desktop/web dashboard.

## Brand Personality

- **Serene & Contemplative**: Calm celestial color tones shifting organically with day/night cycles.
- **Tactile & Refined**: Fluid liquid glass materials, specular ice borders, and Apple-grade micro-motion.
- **Authentic & Dignified**: Rooted in sacred traditions, respectful typography (Vazirmatn & Amiri), and authentic Hadiths.

## Anti-references

- Loud commercial banners, ads, or donation popups.
- Generic AI SaaS templates (flat identical card grids, gradient text, side-stripe cards).
- Overstimulating high-saturation neon colors or distracting push alerts.
- Cluttered spreadsheet tables or low-contrast text on bright backgrounds.

## Design Principles

1. **Sacred Ambiance & Atmospheric Light**: The interface breathes and changes with the actual sky phase (dawn, morning, noon, afternoon, sunset, evening, night, midnight).
2. **Tactile Liquid Glass Depth**: Interfaces use layered materials, optical border highlights, and subtle spring scales rather than flat generic containers.
3. **Tabular Precision & Zero Jitter**: Numerical countdowns, clock figures, and verse numbers use fixed-width tabular figures to preserve visual stillness.
4. **Glanceable & Unobtrusive**: Key information (next prayer countdown and celestial phase) is readable in 200ms from the macOS topbar or dashboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Persian RTL (Right-to-Left) typography with optical spacing for Persian/Arabic character forms.
- High-contrast text legibility tested for both dark midnight backgrounds and golden sunset scenes.
- Tabular figures (`FontFeature.tabularFigures()`) across all active timers.
- Minimum 40×40px touch targets and full keyboard/screen-reader compatibility.
